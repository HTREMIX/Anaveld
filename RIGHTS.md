# Anaveld — Rights and Attribution

Anaveld publishes physics problem studies, model explorations, solutions, and scholarly commentary as **public PDF publications**. Its production sources and publishing tools are not distributed by default.

## Default content license

Original scholarly materials for which Anaveld's respective rights holders can grant permissions are licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), unless a specific publication says otherwise. See [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md).

This generally includes original explanatory text, solutions, commentary, and original diagrams when they are identified as part of the licensed material.

## Publication-level credits

Each PDF should identify its authors, title, publication ID or version when available, and any material from other sources. Its specific rights notice takes precedence when it states an exception to the repository's default license.

A suggested citation is:

> [Author(s)]. *[Publication title]*. Anaveld, [publication ID], [version/year], [URL].

This is a recommended academic citation format, not an additional restriction on rights already granted under CC BY 4.0.

## Excluded or separately licensed material

- **Third-party materials:** Original sources, permissions, and license requirements still apply. Anaveld does not relicense material it does not have authority to license.
- **Cover artwork:** Not included in the default CC BY 4.0 grant unless expressly indicated for that artwork.
- **Brand identity:** CC BY 4.0 does not grant trademark rights or permission to describe modified or independent publications as official Anaveld releases.
- **Unpublished production sources:** LaTeX, Python, editable design assets, templates, and publishing tools are not made available through this repository.

## Independent research

The CC BY 4.0 license concerns copyrightable expression, not ownership of physical laws, mathematical ideas, or general scientific concepts. Scholarly citation of relevant sources is encouraged even when copyright permission is not required.
